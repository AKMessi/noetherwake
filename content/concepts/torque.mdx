## Intuition

A force turns an object most effectively when applied far from the axis and perpendicular to the lever arm. Torque records this rotational effect with a signed direction.

## Formalism

$$
\tau=rF\sin\theta
$$

A small angular turn moves the application point by a tangential distance r dtheta. Work equals tangential force times that distance, so work per angle is torque.

[Explore vectors](concept:vectors). [Explore work](concept:work).

## Worked example

A perpendicular force 3 N acts at lever arm 4 m. Find torque magnitude.

Start with the defining relationship. A small angular turn moves the application point by a tangential distance r dtheta. Work equals tangential force times that distance, so work per angle is torque. Substitute the given values: **`3*4` = 12 N*m**. Keep the unit through the calculation and check whether the sign and scale fit the situation.

## Where it breaks

Choose an axis and sign convention. Torque and energy share dimensions but have different physical meanings.

A common trap is: **using the full lever length when force is radial**. Find the perpendicular distance from the axis to the force line of action.
