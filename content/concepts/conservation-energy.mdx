## Intuition

When only conservative forces do work, kinetic and potential energy trade places while their sum stays fixed. A falling object loses height energy at the rate it gains motion energy.

## Formalism

$$K_i+U_i=K_f+U_f$$

Net work equals kinetic change, while conservative work is minus potential change. Equating them makes the total change zero.

[Explore kinetic energy](concept:kinetic-energy). [Explore potential energy](concept:potential-energy).

## Worked example

Total mechanical energy is 3+4 J and potential energy is 4 J. Find kinetic energy.

Start with the defining relationship. Net work equals kinetic change, while conservative work is minus potential change. Equating them makes the total change zero. Substitute the given values: **`3` = 3 J**. Keep the unit through the calculation and check whether the sign and scale fit the situation.

## Where it breaks

Friction, external work or deformation can change mechanical energy; total energy requires including those channels.

A common trap is: **conserving kinetic energy by itself during a fall**. Track the sum of kinetic and potential terms between two chosen states.
