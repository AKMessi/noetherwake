## Intuition

Electric charge is a signed property that determines electric interaction. Positive and negative contributions add algebraically; an isolated system can redistribute charge without changing its total.

## Formalism

$$
Q_{total}=\sum_i q_i
$$

Count charge entering and leaving a chosen boundary. Equal internal transfers change two parts oppositely, leaving the system total unchanged.

## Worked example

Charges +3 C and -4 C combine. Find net charge.

Start with the defining relationship. Count charge entering and leaving a chosen boundary. Equal internal transfers change two parts oppositely, leaving the system total unchanged. Substitute the given values: **`3-4` = -1 C**. Keep the unit through the calculation and check whether the sign and scale fit the situation.

## Where it breaks

Charge is quantized in elementary units; a continuous density is a large-number approximation. Neutral objects can still polarize.

A common trap is: **neutral means no charged constituents**. Neutrality is equal total positive and negative charge, not absence of either.
