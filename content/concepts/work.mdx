## Intuition

Work measures energy transferred by a force along displacement. Only the aligned component contributes; a perpendicular force can turn motion without doing work.

## Formalism

$$
W=\int\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r
$$

A small aligned push transfers force times displacement. Divide a path into short segments, project the force on each, then sum and refine to an integral.

[Explore dot product](concept:dot-product). [Explore integral](concept:integral). [Explore newton 2](concept:newton-2).

## Worked example

A parallel constant force 3 N moves an object 4 m. Find work.

Start with the defining relationship. A small aligned push transfers force times displacement. Divide a path into short segments, project the force on each, then sum and refine to an integral. Substitute the given values: **`3*4` = 12 J**. Keep the unit through the calculation and check whether the sign and scale fit the situation.

## Where it breaks

Work can be negative. A force perpendicular to every displacement does zero work even if its magnitude is large.

A common trap is: **multiplying force magnitude by any path length**. Use the force component along the displacement, including its sign.
