## Intuition

A projectile combines horizontal motion with vertical free fall. Gravity changes only the vertical component when air resistance is ignored; both components share the same clock.

## Formalism

$$
x=v_0\cos\theta\,t,\qquad y=v_0\sin\theta\,t-\tfrac12gt^2
$$

Project the launch velocity onto two axes. Apply zero acceleration horizontally and negative g vertically, then integrate each component.

[Explore free fall](concept:free-fall). [Explore vectors](concept:vectors). [Explore trig](concept:trig).

## Worked example

Horizontal velocity is 3 m/s. Flight lasts 4 s. Find range.

Start with the defining relationship. Project the launch velocity onto two axes. Apply zero acceleration horizontally and negative g vertically, then integrate each component. Substitute the given values: **`3*4` = 12 m**. Keep the unit through the calculation and check whether the sign and scale fit the situation.

## Where it breaks

The familiar 45-degree maximum range assumes equal launch and landing heights, no air resistance and fixed launch speed.

A common trap is: **gravity gradually removes horizontal velocity**. Separate horizontal and vertical forces before writing component acceleration.
