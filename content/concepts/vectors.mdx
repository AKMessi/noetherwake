## Intuition

A vector carries both size and direction. Components record independent changes along chosen axes, so vector addition is just combining those changes axis by axis.

## Formalism

$$
\mathbf a+\mathbf b=(a_x+b_x,a_y+b_y)
$$

Place the second arrow at the head of the first. The final displacement equals the sum of horizontal and vertical movements.

[Explore trig](concept:trig). [Explore signed numbers](concept:signed-numbers).

## Worked example

Add (3,2) and (4,3). Enter the x-component.

Start with the defining relationship. Place the second arrow at the head of the first. The final displacement equals the sum of horizontal and vertical movements. Substitute the given values: **`3+4` = 7 **. Keep the unit through the calculation and check whether the sign and scale fit the situation.

## Where it breaks

Components depend on the coordinate axes; the physical arrow does not. A vector magnitude is never a signed component.

A common trap is: **adding magnitudes for nonparallel vectors**. Add each component first, then take the length of the resulting vector.
