## Intuition

A free-body diagram isolates one object and lists interactions acting on it. Contact forces and long-range forces enter the same vector sum, while forces exerted by the object belong on another diagram.

## Formalism

$$\sum F_x=ma_x,\qquad\sum F_y=ma_y$$

Choose the body and axes, identify external interactions, resolve components, and apply Newton second law separately along each axis.

[Explore newton 2](concept:newton-2). [Explore vectors](concept:vectors).

## Worked example

A 3 kg body rests on a horizontal table. Use g=10. Find the normal force.

Start with the defining relationship. Choose the body and axes, identify external interactions, resolve components, and apply Newton second law separately along each axis. Substitute the given values: **`10*3` = 30 N**. Keep the unit through the calculation and check whether the sign and scale fit the situation.

## Where it breaks

Normal force need not equal weight. An accelerating lift or an angled push changes the vertical force balance.

A common trap is: **drawing both action and reaction on one body**. Label the object receiving each force; third-law partners act on different bodies.
