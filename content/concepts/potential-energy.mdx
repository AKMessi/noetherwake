## Intuition

Potential energy records recoverable energy of an arrangement. Choose its change so that work done by a conservative force decreases potential energy by the same amount.

## Formalism

$$\Delta U=-\int\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r,\qquad F_x=-dU/dx$$

Near Earth, gravity is downward and constant. Raising a mass by h makes gravitational work -mgh, so potential energy rises by mgh.

[Explore work](concept:work). [Explore integral](concept:integral).

## Worked example

Use g=10. Raise 3 kg through 4 m. Find potential energy increase.

Start with the defining relationship. Near Earth, gravity is downward and constant. Raising a mass by h makes gravitational work -mgh, so potential energy rises by mgh. Substitute the given values: **`10*3*4` = 120 J**. Keep the unit through the calculation and check whether the sign and scale fit the situation.

## Where it breaks

The zero of potential is arbitrary. A single-valued potential cannot represent dissipative friction over closed paths.

A common trap is: **treating the zero of potential as physically fixed**. Shift every potential value by one constant and check that all work predictions remain unchanged.
