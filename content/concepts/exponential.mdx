## Intuition

Exponential growth changes in proportion to what is already present. Equal time intervals multiply the amount by the same factor. The natural base makes the instantaneous rate equal the amount itself.

## Formalism

$$\frac{d}{dx}e^{kx}=ke^{kx}$$

The growth law y prime equals ky says each small interval adds k times the current amount times the interval length. Repeated small multiplications converge to an exponential.

[Explore exponents](concept:exponents). [Explore chain rule](concept:chain-rule).

## Worked example

For f(x)=exp(3x), find f prime at x=0.

Repeated small multiplications converge to an exponential. Substitute the given values: **`3` = 3 **. Keep the unit through the calculation and check whether the sign and scale fit the situation.

## Where it breaks

A constant growth fraction is a model assumption. Resource limits make long-term unlimited growth unrealistic.

A common trap is: **confusing constant addition with constant percentage growth**. Compare equal time intervals: differences are fixed for linear growth, ratios for exponential growth.
