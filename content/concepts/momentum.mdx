## Intuition

Momentum combines mass and velocity into the quantity force changes. In a collection of bodies, internal forces cancel in pairs, leaving external force to change total momentum.

## Formalism

$$\mathbf p=m\mathbf v,\qquad\frac{d\mathbf P}{dt}=\mathbf F_{ext}$$

Add Newton equations for every body. Equal and opposite internal forces vanish from the sum, so an isolated system has constant total momentum.

[Explore newton 2](concept:newton-2). [Explore vectors](concept:vectors).

## Worked example

Mass 3 kg moves at 4 m/s right. Find momentum.

Start with the defining relationship. Add Newton equations for every body. Equal and opposite internal forces vanish from the sum, so an isolated system has constant total momentum. Substitute the given values: **`3*4` = 12 kg*m/s**. Keep the unit through the calculation and check whether the sign and scale fit the situation.

## Where it breaks

Momentum is a vector; opposite velocities can cancel. Conservation requires negligible external impulse over the chosen interval.

A common trap is: **adding speeds instead of signed momenta**. Choose an axis, give each velocity a sign, and sum mass times velocity.
