## Intuition

An inequality names a region of possible values rather than one balance point. Multiplication by a negative number reflects the number line, reversing left and right.

## Formalism

$$a<b\;\Rightarrow\;-a>-b$$

Test the order with two concrete numbers. Their negatives appear in the opposite order on the same axis.

[Explore equations](concept:equations).

## Worked example

For -3x < -3*4, what is the boundary value of x?

Start with the defining relationship. Test the order with two concrete numbers. Their negatives appear in the opposite order on the same axis. Substitute the given values: **`4` = 4 **. Keep the unit through the calculation and check whether the sign and scale fit the situation.

## Where it breaks

Squaring preserves order only with appropriate sign restrictions. The square function folds both sides of zero together.

A common trap is: **keeping the inequality sign when dividing by a negative**. Place -2 and -5 on a number line; the reflected order explains the flip.
