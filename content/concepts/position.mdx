## Intuition

Position names where an object is relative to a chosen origin. Displacement compares the final position with the initial one; changing the origin shifts both and leaves the displacement unchanged.

## Formalism

$$
\Delta x=x_f-x_i
$$

Write final and initial coordinates in the same frame. Their difference cancels the arbitrary origin offset.

[Explore coordinates](concept:coordinates). [Explore units](concept:units).

## Worked example

An object moves from -3 m to 4 m. Find displacement.

Start with the defining relationship. Write final and initial coordinates in the same frame. Their difference cancels the arbitrary origin offset. Substitute the given values: **`3+4` = 7 m**. Keep the unit through the calculation and check whether the sign and scale fit the situation.

## Where it breaks

Displacement is not distance travelled. A round trip can have zero displacement and substantial path length.

A common trap is: **confusing distance with displacement**. Mark the endpoints separately from the path taken between them.
