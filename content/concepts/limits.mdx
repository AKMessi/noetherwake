## Intuition

A limit asks where outputs head as inputs approach a point. It deliberately separates nearby behavior from the value written at the point itself.

## Formalism

$$
\lim_{x\to a}f(x)=L
$$

For a polynomial, each arithmetic operation preserves small changes, so approaching a forces the output toward its substituted value. A removable hole can have a limit despite being undefined.

[Explore functions](concept:functions). [Explore inequalities](concept:inequalities).

## Worked example

Find the limit of 3x+4 as x approaches 2.

Start with the defining relationship. For a polynomial, each arithmetic operation preserves small changes, so approaching a forces the output toward its substituted value. A removable hole can have a limit despite being undefined. Substitute the given values: **`2*3+4` = 10 **. Keep the unit through the calculation and check whether the sign and scale fit the situation.

## Where it breaks

Left and right approaches must agree. A vertical blow-up is not a finite limit; oscillation can prevent any destination.

A common trap is: **equating the limit with the value at a hole**. Factor the expression away from the excluded point, then approach without substituting into zero divided by zero.
