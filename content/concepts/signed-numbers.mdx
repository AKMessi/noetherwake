## Intuition

A sign encodes direction relative to a chosen zero. Debt and credit, left and right, below and above can use the same number line. Subtracting a movement means undoing it.

## Formalism

$$a-(-b)=a+b$$

The opposite of a number cancels it. Undoing a negative displacement therefore moves in the positive direction.

[Explore fractions](concept:fractions).

## Worked example

Start at -3 and move 4 units right. Where do you finish?

Start with the defining relationship. The opposite of a number cancels it. Undoing a negative displacement therefore moves in the positive direction. Substitute the given values: **`-3+4` = 1 **. Keep the unit through the calculation and check whether the sign and scale fit the situation.

## Where it breaks

A negative sign may mean a negative value or an operation; parentheses distinguish the two.

A common trap is: **two negative signs always give a negative**. Read subtraction as adding the opposite, then place the movement on a number line.
