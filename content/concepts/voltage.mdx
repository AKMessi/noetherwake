## Intuition

A voltage difference says how much energy changes per unit charge moved between terminals. A battery maintains this difference by converting chemical energy as charge circulates.

## Formalism

$$\Delta U=q\Delta V$$

Potential is energy divided by charge. Multiplying the terminal difference by transferred charge gives transferred energy.

[Explore electric potential](concept:electric-potential). [Explore charge](concept:charge).

## Worked example

Charge 3 C passes through a 4 V rise. Find energy gained.

Start with the defining relationship. Potential is energy divided by charge. Multiplying the terminal difference by transferred charge gives transferred energy. Substitute the given values: **`3*4` = 12 J**. Keep the unit through the calculation and check whether the sign and scale fit the situation.

## Where it breaks

Voltage is measured between two points. It is not a substance consumed in a wire, and its sign depends on terminal order.

A common trap is: **voltage flows through a component**. Charge flows; a voltage difference describes an energy change per charge between endpoints.
