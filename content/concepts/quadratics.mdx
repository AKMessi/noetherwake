## Intuition

A square grows by more with each equal step away from its minimum. Completing a square reveals the centre of that growth, converting an expanded expression into a shifted parabola.

## Formalism

$$x^2+2hx+h^2=(x+h)^2$$

A square of side x+h contains an x-square, two rectangles of area hx and a corner of area h squared.

[Explore exponents](concept:exponents). [Explore equations](concept:equations).

## Worked example

What constant completes x^2 + 2*3x into a perfect square?

Start with the defining relationship. A square of side x+h contains an x-square, two rectangles of area hx and a corner of area h squared. Substitute the given values: **`3^2` = 9 **. Keep the unit through the calculation and check whether the sign and scale fit the situation.

## Where it breaks

A real quadratic may have no real roots. Taking a square root introduces both signs when solving an equation.

A common trap is: **forgetting the negative square root**. Check both +r and -r in x squared equals r squared.
