## Intuition

Coordinates turn location into numbers relative to an origin and axes. The distance between two points depends on the change along each axis, not their absolute addresses.

## Formalism

$$
d=\sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2+(y_2-y_1)^2}
$$

The horizontal and vertical differences form perpendicular sides of a right triangle. Pythagoras supplies the straight-line distance.

[Explore signed numbers](concept:signed-numbers). [Explore exponents](concept:exponents).

## Worked example

Find the distance from (0,0) to (3,4).

Start with the defining relationship. The horizontal and vertical differences form perpendicular sides of a right triangle. Pythagoras supplies the straight-line distance. Substitute the given values: **`sqrt(3^2+4^2)` = 5 **. Keep the unit through the calculation and check whether the sign and scale fit the situation.

## Where it breaks

This formula assumes flat Euclidean space and matching axis units. A map projection can distort physical distances.

A common trap is: **adding coordinates instead of subtracting**. Translate both points by the same vector: their separation must stay unchanged.
