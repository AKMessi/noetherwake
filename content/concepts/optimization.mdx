## Intuition

At an interior smooth maximum or minimum, a function stops increasing before changing direction. The derivative identifies candidates; the surrounding shape decides what kind they are.

## Formalism

$$
f\prime(x_*)=0\quad\text{at an interior differentiable extremum}
$$

If the derivative were positive at a supposed interior maximum, a small step right would increase the value. A negative derivative would allow a step left.

[Explore power rule](concept:power-rule). [Explore inequalities](concept:inequalities).

## Worked example

Find the x maximizing -(x-3)^2+4.

Start with the defining relationship. If the derivative were positive at a supposed interior maximum, a small step right would increase the value. A negative derivative would allow a step left. Substitute the given values: **`3` = 3 **. Keep the unit through the calculation and check whether the sign and scale fit the situation.

## Where it breaks

Endpoints and corners may also be extrema. A zero derivative can be a flat crossing, so check sign changes or curvature.

A common trap is: **calling every stationary point a maximum**. Compare derivative signs on both sides of the candidate.
