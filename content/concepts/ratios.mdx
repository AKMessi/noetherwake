## Intuition

A ratio describes how two quantities scale together. A constant price per kilogram lets you predict any purchase without memorizing a table. Scaling both quantities equally preserves the ratio.

## Formalism

$$
y=kx
$$

Divide the output by the input to find the constant of proportionality. Multiplication rebuilds the output for a new input.

[Explore fractions](concept:fractions).

## Worked example

3 identical parts cost 4 currency units. What do 6 parts cost?

Start with the defining relationship. Divide the output by the input to find the constant of proportionality. Multiplication rebuilds the output for a new input. Substitute the given values: **`6*4/3` = 8 **. Keep the unit through the calculation and check whether the sign and scale fit the situation.

## Where it breaks

Proportionality requires a fixed ratio and passes through zero. A taxi fare with a starting fee is not proportional.

A common trap is: **adding the same amount preserves a ratio**. Double both members of a ratio and compare their quotient.
