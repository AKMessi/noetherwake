## Intuition

Electric potential is potential energy per unit charge. It records how much work per positive charge is needed to move against the electric field between points.

## Formalism

$$\Delta V=-\int\mathbf E\cdot d\mathbf l,\qquad U=qV$$

Divide conservative electric work by the moved charge. Since force is qE, the charge cancels and the potential difference depends on the field path integral.

[Explore electric field](concept:electric-field). [Explore potential energy](concept:potential-energy). [Explore integral](concept:integral).

## Worked example

Uniform field is 3 N/C right. Move 4 m right. Find potential change.

Start with the defining relationship. Divide conservative electric work by the moved charge. Since force is qE, the charge cancels and the potential difference depends on the field path integral. Substitute the given values: **`-3*4` = -12 V**. Keep the unit through the calculation and check whether the sign and scale fit the situation.

## Where it breaks

Potential has an arbitrary zero, while differences are observable. Time-varying magnetic fields can make electric work path-dependent.

A common trap is: **zero potential implies zero field**. A potential can cross zero while still having a nonzero slope; field depends on slope.
