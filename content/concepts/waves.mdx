## Intuition

A travelling wave shifts the same shape across space. Individual pieces of a medium may only oscillate locally while phase and energy move onward.

## Formalism

$$y(x,t)=A\sin(kx-\omega t),\quad v=\omega/k=f\lambda$$

Hold the phase kx-omega t constant to follow one crest. Its position changes at omega/k; one cycle travels one wavelength in one period.

[Explore oscillator](concept:oscillator). [Explore functions](concept:functions).

## Worked example

Frequency is 3 Hz and wavelength 4 m. Find wave speed.

Start with the defining relationship. Hold the phase kx-omega t constant to follow one crest. Its position changes at omega/k; one cycle travels one wavelength in one period. Substitute the given values: **`3*4` = 12 m/s**. Keep the unit through the calculation and check whether the sign and scale fit the situation.

## Where it breaks

Phase speed differs from particle speed and may differ from group speed in dispersive media.

A common trap is: **the medium must travel with the crest**. Track one marked point of the medium and a separate marked crest.
