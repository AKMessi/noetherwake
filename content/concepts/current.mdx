## Intuition

Current measures charge crossing a section per time. In steady state, charge cannot keep piling up at a junction, so total current entering equals total current leaving.

## Formalism

$$I=\frac{dQ}{dt},\qquad\sum I_{in}=\sum I_{out}$$

Apply charge conservation to a tiny region around a junction. Zero rate of stored charge forces the difference between inflow and outflow to vanish.

[Explore charge](concept:charge). [Explore derivative](concept:derivative).

## Worked example

3 C crosses a section in 4 s. Find average current.

Start with the defining relationship. Apply charge conservation to a tiny region around a junction. Zero rate of stored charge forces the difference between inflow and outflow to vanish. Substitute the given values: **`3/4` = 0.75 A**. Keep the unit through the calculation and check whether the sign and scale fit the situation.

## Where it breaks

Conventional current follows positive charge flow; electrons in a metal drift oppositely. Transient charge storage requires including dQ/dt.

A common trap is: **current is used up by a resistor**. Compare charge entering and leaving the resistor in steady state; energy changes, not net charge.
