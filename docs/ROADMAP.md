# Delivery plan and remaining scope

Milestones are executed in AGENTS.md order. This repository targets a substantial v1 vertical slice, not a claim to have finished all undergraduate mathematics and physics.

1. Specify graph, Bayesian inference, memory, diagnostic, planner and content; commit before code.
2. Implement pure engine and reproducible synthetic benchmark; inspect measured results before UI.
3. Author at least 60 connected algebra→calculus→mechanics→electrostatics/circuits concepts and validate all generators.
4. Build Noetherwake app, local persistence, map, assessment, learning instruments, accessibility and offline support.
5. Run all verification, publish actual metrics and explicit limitations, commit final report.

Deferred breadth: multivariable calculus, linear algebra, ODE systems, probability/statistics, real analysis, thermodynamics, full waves, magnetism/Maxwell, relativity and quantum need complete reviewed lesson banks before they become selectable goals. A graph full of locked empty nodes is not curriculum. Optional BYO-key AI is deferred; no key collection or tutor button is presented. No telemetry or aggregate upload exists, so no data leaves the device. Cross-device sync and push notifications are outside offline v1; reviews appear when the app is opened. See final ranked priorities after evaluation.

## Ranked next steps by learner impact

1. **Independent mathematical and pedagogical review of all 67 lessons.** Solve every family, challenge units and domain restrictions, and replace weak hints or distractors with explanations of observed student errors.
2. **Run a consented human pilot with delayed retention and transfer outcomes.** Synthetic speed improvements are useful engineering checks; they cannot establish whether people learn more.
3. **Improve diagnostic inference on heterogeneous knowledge islands.** Move beyond one depth frontier, calibrate prerequisite conditional probabilities, and reduce unnecessary foundational confirmation without hiding uncertainty.
4. **Expand conceptual and transfer item families.** More contexts, negative/zero cases, multistep tasks, free-body construction, two-dimensional point placement and domain-aware symbolic equivalence have more value than more coefficient variants.
5. **Calibrate memory, item difficulty and uncertainty.** Compare the exponential half-life model against FSRS using local evidence; validate intervals, hint penalties, slip/guess and frustration rules before claiming optimal decisions.
6. **Build richer linked teaching instruments.** Direct slope dragging, energy bookkeeping, vector field probes and circuit current visualization; measure learning outcomes and frame budgets on real phones.
7. **Add linear algebra and multivariable calculus as complete reviewed strands.** Preserve physics-to-math diagnosis, prerequisite checks and build-failing content contracts while extending the graph.
8. **Develop a serious proof track.** Real-analysis definitions, quantifier errors, proof-step rubrics and proof skeletons need a richer checker than the current constrained completion item.
9. **Expand physics through full waves, thermodynamics, magnetism/Maxwell, relativity and quantum.** Include mathematical bridges and derived models before exposing goals, rather than adding empty map nodes.
10. **Test the local-first experience with real assistive technology and devices.** Screen-reader math, touch ergonomics, service-worker updates, persistence recovery and migration must be validated beyond automated Chromium checks.

## Known v1 limitations

The memory parameters, difficulty priors and displayed uncertainty intervals are engineering choices without human calibration. Sessions have an item/time budget, not a promise that every learner finishes at the same second. The diagnostic uses goal-biased graph depth, not a full joint posterior over every prerequisite. Every concept has a parameter-linked visual instrument, but many are general relationship plots rather than bespoke physical simulations. The proof and point tasks are constrained. Free-text explanations use keyword groups and never award mastery. Optional AI, aggregate-data upload, push notifications and cross-device sync are deliberately absent. Automated axe and throttled desktop performance checks do not certify every WCAG criterion or physical-device frame rate.

## Working Page follow-ups and explicit limits

1. **Real-device and assistive-technology validation.** Desktop Chromium/WebKit, native keyboard controls, axe, and CDP touch checks do not replace physical phones, OS file-picker operation, VoiceOver/NVDA/JAWS, browser zoom, or human usability work. At a dense Fit overview, use the full-size List alternative for reliable node targets; zoom necessarily reduces the SVG's visual hit regions.
2. **Calibrated confidence and lesson-time contracts.** The engine exposes per-concept squared confidence error, not a population calibration curve or a validated minimum sample threshold. Lessons have no authored durations. Neither a curve, a threshold message, nor invented durations are displayed.
3. **Paragraph-level authoring metadata.** Definitions and self-checks currently have concept-level metadata. Connections and the authored misconception check are grouped once; anchored per-paragraph notes, reference-aware equation numbers, and colour-marked unknowns need explicit authoring metadata rather than a heuristic rewrite of all MDX. Marked derivations already reveal actual authored lines and align their justifications with the margin.
4. **Persist unfinished practice drafts across reload.** Drafts survive navigation within the current tab. Completed responses, evidence, sessions and reflections survive reload. A durable unfinished answer, its timer and an in-progress session summary require a separately validated persistence contract; no version-1 schema migration was introduced. Placement does resume after reload through a local UI marker and the existing diagnostic state.
5. **Historical snapshots for truncated imports.** Session completion captures exact before/after knowledge in memory. Field notes reconstruct retained answers only when replay through `observe` reproduces the stored concept state. Incomplete histories explicitly withhold historical marks; stored session deltas remain visible. Future exports could carry validated snapshots in a new schema version.
6. **Per-answer notes.** The existing concept-authored self-explanation remains a formative keyword check. Persisted reflections remain attached to completed sessions. There is no pretend per-answer “Save note” control.
7. **Continue content and instrument development.** More bespoke tangent, energy and circuit instruments, independent review beyond the eight expanded lessons, and consented delayed-retention studies remain priorities. Synthetic evidence does not prove human learning effectiveness.
8. **Continue cold-start profiling.** Meaningful generic text renders from static HTML while local learner records are read. Performance reports separate LCP from control readiness; reducing the client catalogue and font transfer can improve interactivity further without replacing real fonts or hiding uncertainty.

The Working Page resolves the historical presentation requests for pointer/pinch atlas panning, a mobile inspector sheet, unified navigation, and an executable Chromium performance gate. Historical screenshot and measurement directories remain available as prior release evidence; they are not measurements of this build.
