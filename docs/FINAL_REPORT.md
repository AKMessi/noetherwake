# Noetherwake delivery report

## What exists

A Next.js App Router / strict TypeScript / Tailwind application, static-exportable without a backend. The deterministic engine is a separate pure TypeScript package. Bayesian tracing, uncertainty, half-life forgetting, direct prerequisite evidence, downward gap hypotheses, adaptive placement, goal-aware recommendations, hint credit, confidence calibration and session budgets are implemented.

The app has goal selection; 12–18-question adaptive placement; a home with one primary next step and its reason; an explorable prerequisite map; explicit locked-node rerouting; worked/faded/independent retrieval; eight item formats; SI dimension checking; symbolic checking; committed predictions; RK4 motion in a worker; annotated derivation steps; reflection and due reviews; light/dark/system appearance; an accessible reading font; validated JSON backups; and versioned offline caching. There is no login, telemetry or remote grading.

The authored slice has **67 connected concepts, 201 generator templates and 67 lesson/instrument specifications**, connecting fractions and algebra through calculus and mechanics to fields, circuits and capacitance. Every build checks 1,000 variants per template (201,000 total), references, DAG/orphans, MDX, KaTeX, units, finite magnitudes, choice answers and generated-answer verification.

## What the simulations show

The initial 160-student sanity benchmark uses three eight-node chains: estimator MAE after 28 static probes is **0.002915**, Brier error **0.000255**, normalized diagnostic frontier error **0.025**. Its goal-aware planner takes 51.8 actions versus 125.8 linear and 359.6 random; this graph intentionally makes relevance useful.

The more informative authored-graph stress test uses 24 matched synthetic students per goal and a 1,000-action cap:

| Goal | Planner actions | Linear actions | Reduction | Planner / linear completion |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Mechanics / energy | 295.6 | 343.8 | 14.0% | 100% / 91.7% |
| Calculus / substitution | 190.8 | 213.1 | 10.5% | 95.8% / 91.7% |
| Electricity / capacitance | 301.6 | 357.6 | 15.7% | 100% / 95.8% |

Random unlocked practice completes 0%, 33.3%, 0% respectively within the cap. Mean action counts include failed/capped runs. These synthetic students have binary latent knowledge, shared response noise and simple learning probabilities; they are not people. ## Verification and limits

See [VERIFICATION.md](VERIFICATION.md) for final test counts, performance measurements and browser coverage. Static assets total about 3.9 MB, including the cached math fonts and deferred checker. The original NTFS workspace stalled; the complete durable repository and milestone commits are in `/home/akmessi/noetherwake`.

This is a working vertical slice, not the entire requested undergraduate catalog or a proven optimal tutor. Independent content review, a human retention/transfer pilot, calibrated parameters, richer diagnostics and bespoke instruments remain necessary. Graph-point assessment is one-dimensional; proof assessment is a constrained skeleton; some visuals are general relation plots. Full advanced physics, real analysis, optional AI, telemetry and push reminders are absent and not presented as working features. The ranked top ten next steps are in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).
